Fieldbook Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Fieldbook ("the app") is made by Joyn Creative ("we"). This page explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

Fieldbook is an offline, local-first app for tracking the places you have been. Your book (stamps, notes, photo references) is stored on your device. You can use the app without an account. This version does not upload your stamps, notes, photos or locations to any server. The free version shows a small number of ads; a one-time purchase removes them.

What stays on your device

  • The places you have stamped, your notes, dates and photo references.
  • Your photos themselves, and any data derived from them.
  • Any location information from your photos. Location is never sent anywhere.

Backups you export are written to storage you choose. Nothing is uploaded as part of exporting.

Photo library (optional)

If you allow photo access, Fieldbook reads the date and location already saved inside your own photos to suggest places you may have visited. It does not look at what the photos show. You approve every suggestion before it is added, and nothing is stamped automatically. If you decline, everything else still works and you can add places by hand. This reading happens only on your device.

Accounts (optional)

You can create an account by entering your email address (we send a one-time code) or by signing in with Google. If you do, we and our authentication provider, Supabase, process:

  • your email address, and for Google sign-in your Google account identifier;
  • a sign-in session stored on your device.

We use this only to sign you in and to run the account. In this version an account does not upload your book. You can sign out at any time.

Deleting your account. In the app, open the account screen and choose "Delete this account". This deletes the account and any data stored for it on our servers. Your book on your device is kept. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing Hello@joyncreative.com from the address you signed up with.

Ads and consent

The free version shows a small number of native ads on some browse screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Before requesting ads, the app shows Google's consent message where the law of your region requires it.

  • We request non-personalized ads. We do not use your places, trips, notes, photos or locations to choose ads, and photo scanning is kept separate from advertising.
  • To serve, measure and protect ads, Google's SDK may process technical information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app details and diagnostics. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • If you buy Lifetime Pro, ads are turned off and no ad requests are made.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app keeps a local record that you own Pro so it works offline.

Children

Fieldbook is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and rights

You control your book: export it, erase it, or uninstall the app to remove it from your device. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold (for example your account email). Email Hello@joyncreative.com to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, for example when backup or sharing features are added, we will update this page and its date before the change ships.
