Fieldbook Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Fieldbook ("the app") is made by Joyn Creative ("we"). This page explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

Fieldbook is an offline, local-first app for tracking the places you have been. Your book is stored on your device, and the app works without an account. Sync is optional, Android-only, and disabled unless a build is configured for it. Signing in alone does not upload your book. When sync is available and you confirm the first-sync review, visits, notes, dates, goals and attached photo metadata (including any saved photo dates and coordinates) are copied to your Supabase account. Original photo files remain on the device; this version does not upload photo display copies or restore images on another device. Optional sign-in, ads, purchases and catalogue updates use network services as described below. The free version shows a small number of ads; a one-time purchase removes them.

What stays on your device

  • The local book remains on this device, whether or not sync is enabled.
  • Original photo files and the results of scanning the photo library.
  • Photo-derived coordinates and timestamps are not sent by the scanner. If a photo is attached to a visit, its saved date and coordinates are included in sync when sync is enabled and confirmed.

Backups you export are written to storage you choose. Nothing is uploaded as part of exporting.

Photo library (optional)

If you allow photo access, Fieldbook reads the date and location already saved inside your own photos to suggest places you may have visited. It does not look at what the photos show. You approve every suggestion before it is added, and nothing is stamped automatically. If you decline, everything else still works and you can add places by hand. This reading happens only on your device.

Catalogue updates (optional)

Fieldbook can check for corrected place names, descriptions and coordinates from a configured catalogue endpoint. This is off by default and only appears in Settings when a build has an endpoint configured. If you turn it on, the app periodically requests catalogue files. It does not include your account, stamps, notes, photos or travel history in those requests. The endpoint receives ordinary network connection metadata, such as an IP address. If you decline, everything else still works and the app uses the catalogue it shipped with.

Accounts (optional)

You can create an account by entering your email address (we send a one-time code) or by signing in with Google. If you do, Supabase processes authentication requests for us. Account information includes:

  • your email address, and for Google sign-in your Google account identifier;
  • a sign-in session stored on your device.

We use this to sign you in and manage the account. Supabase also receives the network requests needed to provide authentication. If sync is enabled in the Android build and you confirm setup, Supabase stores the visits, notes, dates, photo metadata and goals included in your book so it can be restored or synced on another device. For attached photos, this includes saved capture dates and coordinates, but not the original image files. The service receives ordinary connection metadata, including an IP address. Scanning your library does not itself send photo data. Turning sync off on a device stops future sync from that device; it does not delete the existing account copy. You can sign out at any time.

Account deletion removes the remote account; it does not erase the local book on this device.

Request account deletion

In Fieldbook, open Settings → Account and choose "Delete this account". To request deletion from the web, email Hello@joyncreative.com from the address used to sign up and ask to delete your Fieldbook account. We delete the account and associated data stored on our servers. The local book remains on each device and can be erased separately in Settings.

Ads and consent

The free version shows a small number of native ads on some browse screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Before requesting ads, the app shows Google's consent message where the law of your region requires it.

  • We request non-personalized ads. We do not use your places, trips, notes, photos or photo-location details to choose ads, and photo scanning is kept separate from advertising.
  • Google's Android SDK disclosure lists data the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions such as launches, taps and ad views, diagnostics, and device/account identifiers such as Advertising ID or App Set ID. Consent and device settings can affect some processing. See Google's SDK disclosure and Google's ads privacy policy.
  • If you buy Lifetime Pro, ads are turned off and no ad requests are made.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google Play processes payment credentials; Fieldbook does not receive your card or bank details. The app receives purchase information needed to grant or restore Pro and keeps a local record of the entitlement so it works offline.

Children

Fieldbook is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and rights

You control your book: export it, erase it, or uninstall the app to remove it from your device. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold (for example your account email). Email Hello@joyncreative.com to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, including if cloud backup or sharing is added, we will update this page and its date before the change ships. Signing in alone does not enable backup or sync; sync requires a configured Android build and explicit confirmation.
