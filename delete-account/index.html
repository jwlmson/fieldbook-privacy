Delete your Fieldbook account

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Fieldbook is made by Joyn Creative. This page explains how to delete your optional Fieldbook account and what happens to your data. For the full privacy policy, see Fieldbook Privacy Policy.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Fieldbook on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete the account and follow the prompts to confirm.

Account deletion removes the remote account; it does not erase the local book on this device.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot use the app, email us from the address you used to sign up:

  • To: Hello@joyncreative.com
  • Subject: Fieldbook account deletion request
  • Include: the email address associated with your Fieldbook account (and, if you signed in with Google, note that in the message).

We delete the account and associated data stored on our servers. The local book remains on each device and can be erased separately in Settings.

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we remove from our servers (including data stored for sync through Supabase):

  • your account and profile (email address, and for Google sign-in your Google account identifier);
  • synced visits, including notes, seats, and ratings;
  • goals;
  • photo records attached to visits, including saved capture dates and location coordinates (not the original image files on your device);
  • friendships;
  • entitlements tied to your account on our servers (for example Pro status used for sync or restore).

What stays on your device

  • The local book remains on your device after account deletion unless you erase it yourself.
  • Original photo files and photo-library scan results stay on the device.
  • A local record of Lifetime Pro may remain so the app can work offline, as described in the privacy policy.

You can export your book, erase it, or uninstall the app to remove it from your device.

Retention

Our privacy policy does not state a fixed number of days to complete account deletion after you submit a request. Deletion applies to account and sync data on our servers as described above. Analytics events sent to PostHog are separate; to request deletion of previously sent usage analytics, email Hello@joyncreative.com (we may need your installation ID). Failed analytics events may remain on the device for up to seven days, capped at 100.
