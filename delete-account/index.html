Delete your Fieldbook account

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Fieldbook is made by Joyn Creative. This page explains how to delete your optional Fieldbook account and what happens to your data. For the full privacy policy, see Fieldbook Privacy Policy.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Fieldbook on your Android device and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Manage account.
  3. Tap Delete this account.
  4. Confirm with Delete the account.

This deletes the account and its synced copy on our servers; the local book stays on your phone.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot use the app, email us from the address you used to sign up:

  • To: Hello@joyncreative.com
  • Subject: Fieldbook account deletion request
  • Include: the email address associated with your Fieldbook account (and, if you signed in with Google, note that in the message).

We delete the account and associated data stored on our servers. The local book remains on each device and can be erased separately in Settings.

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we remove from our servers (including data stored for sync through Supabase):

  • your account (email address, and for Google sign-in your Google account identifier, name and profile-photo link);
  • synced visits, including place, date, notes, seat details and ratings, and deletion markers;
  • goals;
  • photo records attached to visits, including saved capture dates and location coordinates (not the original image files on your device);
  • any other data linked to your account on our servers.

What stays on your device

  • The local book remains on your device after account deletion unless you erase it yourself.
  • Original photo files and photo-library scan results stay on the device.

You can export your book, erase it, or uninstall the app to remove it from your device.

Retention

Deleting the account in the app removes the account and its synced data from our servers when you confirm. For requests sent by email, our privacy policy does not state a fixed number of days to complete deletion. Deletion applies to account and sync data on our servers as described above. Fieldbook does not send usage analytics, so there is no separate analytics data to delete.
